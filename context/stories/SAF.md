## SAF · Trust and safety

### SAF-01 Accept terms (M5)
- AC1 Sign up requires accepting the terms and community guidelines, which include the no-objectionable-content clause (App Store 1.2). `e2e`

### SAF-02 Report a profile, gig or message (M5)
- AC1 Report is on every profile, gig and message, with reasons. Reports reach the team page and email the team. `db` `e2e`

### SAF-03 Block someone (M5)
- AC1 Blocking hides each person from the other in search, gigs and messages, and stops new invites and applications between them. `db` `e2e`
